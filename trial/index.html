About the platform

Pharma Data Exchange helps research organizations standardize how they store, govern, and share laboratory data. The goal is to make every dataset traceable, searchable, and ready for downstream modeling.

Governance & compliance

Role-based access control, audit trails, and approvals keep sensitive datasets secure.

Metadata-first

Harmonized metadata and standardized fields make datasets AI-ready.
